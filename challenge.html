Challenge it.

What we want to know

  • Where are the numbers wrong?
  • Which policies won't work?
  • What have we missed?
  • Which ideas should be strengthened?
  • What should be removed?

The objective isn't to defend every sentence. The objective is to find a better settlement for Britain.
