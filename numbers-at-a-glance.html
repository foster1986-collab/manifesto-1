PART XX · MANIFESTO 1.1

The Numbers at a Glance.

The principal commitments, targets, rates and programme figures in one accessible summary.

Read Budget 3.1 Budget & Numbers

Important context

This page summarises the stated Manifesto 1.1 position. It does not convert working estimates into independently certified costings.

Where the manifesto identifies an unresolved question, illustrative assumption or requirement for further modelling, that qualification is retained.

Income, taxation and participation

Income, taxation and participation commitments
Commitment Manifesto 1.1 position
USI starting point £4,000 a year
USI year-10 target £12,570 a year
USI eligibility age 21 and over
Participation requirement 16 hours a week, averaged over the year, for people capable of participating
Employee National Insurance Abolished and replaced by the unified income-tax system
Employer National Insurance Reduced from the 15% source baseline to 10% over ten years
Proposed income-tax rates 33%, 40%, 45%, 47.5% and 49%
Defence levy 1% of gross income
National Care levy 1% of gross income

Defence, housing and education

Defence, housing and education commitments
Commitment Manifesto 1.1 position
Defence target 5% of GDP within five years
Wartime mobilisation level A potential 8% of GDP mobilisation level, not a peacetime spending pledge
Housing One million additional homes over ten years through mixed tenure
Housing delivery Councils, housing associations, partnerships and private builders
USI participation-age benchmark Age 21, separate from the guarantee for people under 21
Education and training guarantee Extends until the 21st birthday; the exact requirement wording remains unresolved
Military route Three years from age 18, with transition support at 21
Education and training bursary Available for approved participation between ages 18 and 21

Pensions, justice and national utilities

Pensions, justice and national-utility commitments
Commitment Manifesto 1.1 position
Workplace pension Compulsory, with an employee contribution and employer match
Government pension tax relief Source proposal of 25% on employee contributions
State-pension target £18,000 annual safety net, means-tested against private pension income
New prisons and capacity Three rehabilitation-focused prisons providing approximately 15,000 places
New courts 125 additional magistrates' courts and 30 additional Crown Courts
Justice infrastructure capital Original estimate of £9-£13 billion, with operating costs treated separately
Water companies Major water companies brought into public ownership

Investment, business and energy

Investment, business and energy commitments
Commitment Manifesto 1.1 position
Relocation incentive 0% Corporation Tax for 24 months, subject to qualifying conditions
Relocation commitment At least ten years, with clawback where the commitment is broken
Energy standing charges Abolished, with legitimate costs recovered through unit pricing
Regional Investment Fund £20 billion annual target, with allocations reviewed annually
Technology Investment Fund £15 billion annual target
Four-day working model Approximately 32 hours a week where appropriate
Public services Increasing seven-day operation
Banking Saturday and Sunday payments

Cannabis and food reform

Cannabis and food-policy commitments
Commitment Manifesto 1.1 position
Cannabis A legal, adult-only regulated market with taxation at three levels
Food classification A mandatory Green, Amber and Red nutritional classification system
Healthy Food Fund Relevant food-tax receipts supporting subsidies and the specified healthy-food and public-health programmes

How to read these numbers

These figures summarise policy commitments rather than proving that every programme has been fully funded or independently costed.

Budget 3.1 distinguishes annual expenditure, gross programme envelopes, capital estimates, loans and illustrative source assumptions. Those categories should not be added together as though they were one final annual spending total.
