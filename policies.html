Manifesto 1.1

30 policies.

The headline commitments in one place. The original 30-policy index is retained for continuity; policies 11 and 13 now link to one consolidated housing programme. These index numbers are separate from the public-release proposal numbers.

01. Universal Standard Income

USI begins at £4,000 and rises progressively towards £12,570 over ten years.

02. 16-Hour Contribution Guarantee

People capable of work normally average 16 hours a week in work, education, training or approved participation.

03. Abolish Employee National Insurance

Employee NI is replaced by a single progressive income-tax system.

04. Progressive Income Tax

Marginal rates of 33%, 40%, 45%, 47.5% and 49%.

05. Reform the Personal Allowance

The allowance progressively falls as the USI rises.

06. Reduce Employer NI

Employer NI falls gradually from 15% to 10% over ten years.

07. Competitive Corporation Tax

Corporation Tax remains broadly stable initially, with reductions where growth permits.

08. Britain Welcome Programme

Qualifying international relocations receive 0% Corporation Tax for 24 months, subject to a ten-year commitment.

09. National Care Service

A 1% gross-income levy funds a safety net for catastrophic and unaffordable care costs.

10. Universal Healthcare Insurance

Compulsory qualifying health insurance with government subsidies.

11. One Million New Homes Through Mixed Tenure

One million additional homes over ten years through social, affordable, intermediate and private delivery.

Day 10 · Revised Proposal 09. Replaces the withdrawn all-social pledge following public scrutiny.

12. Planning Reform

A national housing and infrastructure test and stronger central intervention.

13. Private Housebuilding Incentives

Conditional incentives for verified additional homes, with measurable delivery conditions and clawback.

Consolidated into Day 10 · Revised Proposal 09, alongside policy 11—not a separate additional housing target.

14. Pragmatic Net Zero

Decarbonisation remains a long-term ambition, guided by affordability, security and competitiveness.

15. Nuclear Britain

Major investment in nuclear, SMRs, research and grid resilience.

16. Domestic Energy Security

Continue developing North Sea oil and gas where appropriate.

17. Social Energy Tariff

Targeted energy support for lower-income households.

18. Abolish Energy Standing Charges

Recover legitimate domestic network and administration costs through unit pricing.

19. Regional Investment Fund

£20bn annual target weighted towards deprivation, productivity and infrastructure need.

20. Defence at 5% of GDP

Progressively raise defence spending to 5% of GDP within five years.

21. 21-Year Guarantee

Every young person remains in education, training, employment, apprenticeship or military service until 21.

22. 18–21 Military Route

A three-year Armed Forces route offering salary, training and transferable skills.

23. Technology Investment Fund

Large-scale public funding for AI, robotics, biotech, fusion, quantum, space and advanced manufacturing.

24. Four-Day Working Model

Move towards approximately 32 hours/four days where appropriate.

25. 24/7 Public Services

Government services increasingly operate seven days a week.

26. Weekend Banking

Modernise payments so salaries and government/business payments can be credited at weekends.

27. Work & Independence Programme

Enhanced support, retraining, workplace adaptation and incentives for returning to work.

28. Reformed Capability Assessments

Assess functional capability and realistic work capacity rather than diagnosis alone.

29. Compulsory Workplace Pensions

Every worker has a portable workplace pension.

30. £18,000 State Pension Safety Net

Long-term state pension target of £18,000, with private pension income taken into account.
