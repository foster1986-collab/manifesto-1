About Manifesto 1.0

An idea before a movement.

Manifesto 1.0 is an independent attempt to start a serious debate about Britain's next economic and social settlement.

Why Manifesto 1.0?

The project begins from a simple premise: Britain does not lack wealth, talent or ambition, but its economic and social settlement increasingly struggles to reconcile security, affordability, work, investment and long-term national resilience.

Manifesto 1.0 brings together income security, work incentives, taxation, housing, healthcare, social care, energy, defence, technology, welfare, pensions and infrastructure.

What it is — and isn't

It is

A policy proposal, an invitation to debate and a framework that can be revised as evidence improves.

It isn't

Not a political party, not an official government costing and not a claim that every policy is already proven.
