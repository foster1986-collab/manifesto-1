A New Settlement for Britain.

Britain does not lack wealth, talent or ambition. What Britain lacks is a coherent economic settlement capable of turning those strengths into sustained prosperity.

Manifesto 1.1 proposes a different settlement: security without dependency, responsibility without abandonment, growth without complacency and long-term reform rather than short-term giveaways.

The proposition

A new social contract.

The citizen receives security. In return, the citizen who is capable of contributing is expected to contribute.

01

Security

Nobody who genuinely cannot work should be abandoned.

02

Responsibility

Those capable of contributing should work, study, train or otherwise participate.

03

Growth

Britain should actively encourage enterprise, investment, productivity, housing and technology.

The evolution to Manifesto 1.1.

We listened. We reconsidered. We changed policy.

Manifesto 1.1 is the natural progression of Manifesto 1.0: a programme tested in public, challenged through debate and revised in response.

The original pledge for one million new social homes has been withdrawn following public scrutiny and citizen involvement. The revised programme aims to enable one million additional homes over ten years through mixed tenure, with public and social delivery alongside conditional private-sector incentives.
